Who We Are

Cooking with Heart Since 2018

EASYBITE started as a small food truck by two culinary school graduates who believed that great food shouldn't be complicated to get. What began as a weekend project quickly grew into a full-fledged restaurant and delivery service.

Today, we serve hundreds of happy customers every day — from office lunches to family dinners — all with the same care and quality that sparked our journey in the first place.

Every dish on our menu is crafted using locally sourced ingredients, traditional recipes with a modern twist, and above all — passion.

🌱

Our Mission

To make restaurant-quality food accessible, affordable, and fast — delivered right to your door.
